Why This Business Works

Busy, successful business owners know they need AI but don't have the time to figure it out. They'll pay a premium to outsource the discovery, decision-making, and implementation — just like they outsource accounting, legal, and insurance. You're not selling software. You're selling expertise, time-bought-back, and a competitive edge.

Three Levers of ROI — Decision Filter

Every recommendation must satisfy at least one. If a tool doesn't pull a lever, don't recommend it.

LeverQuestionExample Tool
EffectivenessDoes it make the client more money?AI lead-gen, upsell agents, smarter proposals
EfficiencyDoes it save measurable time?SaneBox (email), Claude Cowork, Claude skills

AOA Framework — Process Transformation

Never skip straight to "automate." Most clients want a 15-step process automated when really they should first cut it to 8.

1
Audit
Document how the work actually happens today, end-to-end. Inputs, outputs, decision points.
2
Optimize
Remove redundant steps. Merge, eliminate, simplify. Cut cycle time before adding tech.
3
Automate
Now turn the optimized process into a Claude skill, N8N flow, or Zapier automation.

4 Ways to Find Clients (No Audience Required)

These all work this week, no followers or money needed.

① Warm Network Mini Assessments

Send the outreach text to 10 small-business owners you know. Expect 6 replies, 3 bookings, 1–2 paid conversions.

Conversion math: 10 texts → 6 replies → 3 bookings → 1 paid ($999) → 1 concierge ($1K+/mo).

② Local AI for Business Meetup

Partner with a co-working space. 90-min event: 30 networking → 30 demo on Claude Cowork → 30 networking. Promote in local Facebook groups. Text attendees 24h later with the free mini offer.

Why it works: Compounds. By month 3–6 you're the "AI guy" in town.

③ Door Knock / Lead Magnet

Target mom-and-pop service businesses with strong Google reviews but weak web presence. Walk in, ask for the owner, offer the free mini on the spot.

Why it works: Fastest path to a client this week. One founder closed his first paid assessment from a single door knock + coffee.

④ Partnership Marketing

Approach accountants, insurance agents, realtors, chamber of commerce. Offer to (a) take their overflow AI questions, or (b) co-host a free AI workshop for their clients.

Why it works: Their clients are your ideal clients. Make them look smart by having an AI resource to point to.

Top Objections & How to Handle Them

Have these responses ready. Click "Copy" to grab any rebuttal.

"Can't I just use Claude or ChatGPT myself?"
The most common objection — feels like it should be free.
"Sure — and I could do my own taxes. I could write my own insurance. But I'm not a CPA, and I'm not an insurance agent. Wouldn't it make more sense to hire someone whose full-time job is staying on top of this stuff, rather than spending your own time and money figuring it out via trial and error?"
"I don't have time to learn this right now."
The irony objection — busy people need this most.
"That's all the more reason. The whole point of AI is to make you money, save you time, and improve quality. You can't afford not to do this. Every day you wait, your competitors are pulling ahead. Let's at least do the free 15-minute assessment so you can see where the leverage is."
"Let me think about it" / "Let me talk to my partner."
The stalling objection.
"I get it — it's a real decision. But every week you wait is another chunk of time spent doing things AI could be doing for you. The cost of getting started is small. The cost of doing nothing? Nothing changes — and your competitors keep moving."
Full 8-objection playbook is a separate free Notion doc Corey mentions in the video. Treat this as the top 3 to memorize cold.
